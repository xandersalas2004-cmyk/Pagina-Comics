About us

At Comics Heroes, we believe that comics are more than just ink and paper—they are portals to infinite universes, legendary heroes, and unforgettable stories. Located right in the heart of CDMX, our mission is to build a vibrant, inclusive space where fans of all ages can discover their next great read.
